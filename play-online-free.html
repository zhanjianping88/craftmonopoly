What happens in a match?

  • Roll two dice around a 28-space board
  • Buy plots and upgrade them when you land on your own property
  • Build biome majorities to increase rent
  • Manage Creeper Tax, event cards, Nightfall, and rising World Storm upkeep

A local board game, not online multiplayer

Craft Monopoly runs in your browser, but players share a device rather than joining over the internet. Set human seats for pass-and-play, add AI for a solo match, or combine both. The game is free to start and does not require registration.

What makes a match

Each turn is a choice between expanding your portfolio and protecting your cash. A two-of-three biome majority boosts rent, while property upgrades make those plots more expensive to land on. Event cards add swings, and rising upkeep means no lead is completely safe.

What you need

Use a modern desktop or mobile browser with an internet connection for the first load. The 3D board assets load from a content delivery network; after that, set up the table and play on one device.

FAQ

Can I play with friends online?

No. Multiplayer is local pass-and-play on one device. You can also use AI opponents.

Do I need to register?

No registration is required to start a local browser session and test the game flow.

Can I use AI opponents?

Yes. Fill table slots with AI builders, or pass the same device between local players.
